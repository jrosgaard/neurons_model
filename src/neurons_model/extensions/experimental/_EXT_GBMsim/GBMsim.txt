GBM sim is experimental and not functional is the current release of the package.


While it's titled as a Glioblastoma Multiforme model, 
its based on a non-specific tumor growth model and can be used to model more than GBM.


It's currently written to use the non-dimensional Kirschner-Panetta model with:

    1. effector cells (x)
    2. tumor cells (y) 
    3. IL-2 concentration (z).
